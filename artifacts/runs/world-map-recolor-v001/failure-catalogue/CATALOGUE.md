## v011 systemic findings (my 4x review)

1. **Coastline erosion + misregistration everywhere.** 11,812 source-coloured pixels (7.8 %) have no
   colour in v011, and 34,625 v011-coloured pixels sit where the source has none (sea painted as land).
   Eaten coasts confirmed by eye: US eastern seaboard, Nova Scotia/Newfoundland (Halifax and
   St John's labels float), West Africa (the "Dakar" label at (430,272) floats — Senegal/Mauritania
   coast retreats ~20 px east), Great Britain (gone), Italy (gone), Peru (Lima label floats),
   SW Australia. Painted sea confirmed by eye: Java Sea/Malacca Strait, Red Sea band, sea south of
   Indonesia swallowed by the displaced blue Australia.
2. **Floating/anchorless labels.** Every city label keeps its source position while the land moved or
   vanished under it: New York, Dakar, Lima, Halifax, St John's, Dubai (over unpainted white
   peninsula), Singapore (over the fused green blob). Several labels are additionally half-wiped by
   the letter heuristics: Perth (48->14 navy px), Paris (171->16), London (168->47, reads "Londo–"),
   Buenos Aires (133->76, reads "…os Aires"), INDIA caps mangled to ":ND:A" at ~(692–720, 258–268),
   navy-on-red and unreadable.
3. **Annotation/plate debris.** A solid red bar with glyph residue at ~(746, 275–300) where the source
   has a yellow vertical badge (recoloured, not wiped); a red "1" plate fragment on Venezuela at
   ~(315,310); blue NE-shaped marker blobs floating at Cape Verde ~(432,265); black outline squiggles
   over France/Germany and inside recolored landmasses (Borneo, Australia) where the NE coastline was
   drawn at the wrong registration; scattered single-colour crumbs in the southern oceans and around
   Tierra del Fuego.
4. **Colour system off-spec.** 31 distinct flat colours (source: ~10 flat + AA); pastels
   (mint/lavender/salmon) that are not in the source palette; micro-countries render as sub-5-px
   confetti, worst across Europe, Central America, the Caucasus and Central Asia.
5. **Gates did not see any of this.** All four shipped versions v008–v011 passed
   "fidelity outside_changed = 0" and "75/75 spot checks" (ledger.md, provenance.json) while items
   1–4 were present: the fidelity gate only checks containment to declared regions and the spot check
   only samples one colour per country — neither measures shape fidelity, label anchoring, palette
   membership, or debris.
