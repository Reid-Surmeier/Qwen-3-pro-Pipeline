# Icon motion variables

Research and experiments should isolate one variable at a time:

- identity: silhouette, topology, internal negative space, stroke joins/caps, typography;
- material: flat vector, paper, glass, metal, pixel art, or other deliberately locked treatment;
- motion: translation, rotation, scale, reveal, draw-on, morph, secondary parts, motion blur;
- dynamics: easing curve, anticipation, overshoot, damping, squash/stretch, settle time;
- timing: duration, beats, first/last holds, loop seam, audiovisual synchronization;
- framing: canvas, safe area, scale, center, camera lock, perspective prohibition;
- background: transparency expectation, solid matte, gradient prohibition, shadow policy;
- temporal consistency: edge shimmer, color pulsing, geometry drift, unwanted object persistence;
- conditioning: first frame, first/last frames, reference image, motion video, reference audio;
- sampling: model/canonical version, seed, resolution, duration, generated audio.

Start with a Mini factorial screen on the most uncertain variable, then transfer the winning motion
brief to 2.5. Do not infer that a Mini seed reproduces identical motion in 2.5.
